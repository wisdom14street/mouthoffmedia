Process

Straight talk, start to finish.

You always know what is happening, what is next and what we need from you.

01

Talk

We start with a call or a message. You tell us what you sell, who buys it and what is not working. We tell you straight what we would do.

02

Plan

We map the pages, the angle and the one action each page should drive. You approve the plan before we build.

03

Build

We design and build mobile-first, then test on phone-sized screens, check every image and fix anything that overflows or loads slowly.

04

Launch

We connect your domain, set up HTTPS, submit the sitemap and make sure link previews look right when people share.

05

Grow

After launch we keep tuning copy, speed and search visibility based on what real visitors do.

What every launch includes

  • Mobile testing on phone-sized screens
  • HTTPS on your own domain
  • sitemap.xml and robots.txt
  • Structured data (JSON-LD)
  • llms.txt for AI assistants
  • Designed 1200x630 link-preview image
  • Custom 404 page
  • Privacy page
