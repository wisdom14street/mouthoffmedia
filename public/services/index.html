Services

Everything your business needs to speak up online.

Pick one service or bring us the whole thing. We handle design, build, launch and visibility.

01

Web design

Custom, fast, mobile-first websites with a voice of their own. No bloated templates. Every page is built to load quickly and to push visitors toward one clear next step.

  • Mobile-first layouts tested on real phone sizes
  • Copy and structure built around how your customers actually buy
  • Fast static hosting where it fits, so you are not paying for servers you do not need
02

Ecommerce and Shopify storefronts

Branded storefronts that sell from your existing Shopify inventory. Multiple sites can share one catalog and one checkout, so you launch new brands without running new stores.

  • Multi-item carts that hand off to Shopify checkout
  • Live prices and stock pulled from your catalog
  • Product grids, filters and sticky carts that work with one thumb
03

Web apps and PWAs

Installable web apps that work on iPhone and Android straight from the browser, with no app-store wait. Add-to-home-screen, offline pages and app-like speed.

  • Web app manifest, icons and service worker
  • Works offline for key pages
  • Dashboards, booking flows and internal tools
04

SEO and AI-search visibility

Technical SEO done properly, plus the newer work of being findable by AI assistants like ChatGPT. Clean markup, structured data, sitemaps and llms.txt.

  • JSON-LD structured data (LocalBusiness, Product, Organization)
  • Sitemaps, canonical tags and robots.txt that welcome AI crawlers
  • Original, niche-specific copy for every page, never duplicated across sites
05

Local business presence

For shops with a door: make sure people searching nearby find you, understand where you are, and walk in.

  • Address, map and walk-in directions on every page
  • Local landing pages that follow Google guidelines
  • Click-to-call and directions buttons above the fold
06

Branding and link-preview images

When someone shares your link in iMessage, WhatsApp or Slack, the preview should look like a brand, not a broken crop. We design the logo, colors and 1200x630 share cards.

  • Open Graph and Twitter card images for every site
  • Logos, app icons and favicons
  • A consistent look across every page and every share
07

Multi-site networks

A family of focused sites, each with its own niche, wording and brand, all honestly disclosed as part of one business and all feeding one inventory.

  • Shared toolkit so updates reach every site at once
  • Each site gets distinct content and a distinct audience
  • Built to stay on the right side of search-engine spam policies
